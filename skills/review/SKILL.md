---
name: review
description: "Read a finished deliverable as its reader would, in a context that never saw how it was made, and mark what only makes sense to someone who watched the work. Use before handing over a design, copy, docs, a PR or a piece of writing, or for /review."
---

Give a fresh subagent only three things: the deliverable, who reads it and what they need, and the brief or spec it was made from if there is one. Nothing from the conversation.

Ask it to mark, with location and a rewrite:

- what refers to the making of it: rejected options, earlier versions, the order things were tried, the requester's own words carried in, rules it says it followed;
- what the reader needs and doesn't get;
- terms the reader hasn't been given;
- where it reads as the common version of this kind of thing (use the list from `research` if there is one);
- wording that readers now recognise as generated, from [references/reads-as-ai.md](references/reads-as-ai.md);
- claims stronger than their support.

Fix in the producing context, then check the fixed version the same way if the changes were large.
