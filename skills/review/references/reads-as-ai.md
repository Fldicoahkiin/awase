# Reads as AI (2026-11)

Readers have seen enough generated text that some wording now tells them who wrote it, whatever the content. This list is about that reader, so it ages: add what readers start to notice, drop what they stop noticing, and keep the date current.

Use it after the content is right. Rewrite the sentence plainly; swapping in a synonym keeps the same shape and the same tell.

## 中文

**把思考说成物理动作**（翻译腔，见鸭哥《写作中的AI味是哪儿来的》，2026-04）：接住、锋利、击穿、拆解、收口、打穿、收紧、扛住、撑不住、不崩、不爆、落地。改成日常说法：收到、记下、不成立、站不住。

**形容词先替读者下结论，再接冒号**：「逻辑很清晰：」「原因很简单：」「问题很直接：」「结论很明确：」。删掉前半句，只留事实。

**抽象名词做主语、评价词收尾**：「工程上的现实比这些数字难看」这类「X 的 Y 比 Z 更 W」。换成具体的人、动作、对象做主语。

**有稳定译法的英文词夹在句子里**：context、state、cache、claim。术语还没统一的（prompt、embedding、harness）可以留。

**防御、免责的口气**：守住（底线、规则）、边界、边界感、兜底、不烧心、稳稳的、放心、别慌、先说结论、说白了、本质上、一句话总结、值得注意的是、需要指出的是、这一步很关键。

**套话和行话**：不是……而是……、不只是、不仅仅、不再只是、真正的 X 不是 Y、破折号连用、无缝、全新、焕新、重磅、赋能、极致、一站式、闭环、抓手、颗粒度、对齐、沉淀、链路、心智。

## English

**Stock words**: delve, foster, leverage, robust, seamless, comprehensive, crucial, genuinely, "it's worth noting", "importantly".

**Framing moves**: "This isn't about X. It's about Y.", "X, not Y" setting up an alternative nobody raised, "Question? Answer.", "Bottom line:", "In short:", "The simplest mental model is:".

**Invented compound labels** and hyphen-stacked adjectives ("exact-head checks", "editorial-row layouts").

**Saying what won't happen** or what stays unchanged, when nobody asked.

Sources: Wikipedia, *Signs of AI writing*; OpenAI's GPT-6 prompting guide (the "slop" paragraph); 鸭哥，《写作中的AI味是哪儿来的》<https://yage.ai/share/ai-chinese-translationese-20260418.html>.
