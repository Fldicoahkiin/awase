---
name: decide
description: "Lay out a choice so the human can make it quickly, then record the decision with its reason, scope and expiry. Use when a trade-off is the human's to make, or for /decide."
---

1. State the decision in one line and why it can't be deferred.
2. Give the options, two to four. For each: what it does, what it costs, what it rules out later, and how easily it can be undone. Prototype or measure where that settles a difference; say what you observed.
3. Recommend one, with the reason.
4. After the human chooses, record it where later work will read it (the project's decision log, or a `decisions.md` next to the work): the decision, the reason, what it applies to, and until when or until what. A choice made for one case stays scoped to that case; the record keeps the next person or agent from treating an exception as the standard.

Later work reads the record, not the conversation that produced it.
