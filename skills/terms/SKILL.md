---
name: terms
description: "Give the human the expert words for what they are describing, borrow from fields that solved the same problem, and restate the request in those words. Use when someone knows what they want but not how the field says it, or for /terms."
---

Work from the human's own files, code and words.

1. If the description fits more than one problem, list the two or three it could be, the term that names each, and the sign that tells them apart. Pick the likely one and say why.
2. Give the terms that change what gets built, most consequential first. For each: the word, one plain sentence on what it means, the part of the request it names, one real project, paper or product that uses it that way, with a link you have opened.
3. Name two or three neighbouring fields that solved the same shape of problem. Say what carries over, where the comparison breaks, and which terms come back from it.
4. Name what the request leaves out that someone in the field would ask about.
5. Restate the request with the new words.

Cut any term or citation you could not confirm.
